## Photography

The starter references the same 24 Unsplash CDN assets as the reference implementation. They are
hotlinked, not bundled. See the reference project's `CREDITS.md` for the full asset inventory. If you
reuse the starter, replace the imagery with visually verified photos for your regions and keep a
licence trail.
