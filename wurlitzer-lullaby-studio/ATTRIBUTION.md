# Attribution

## Origin

Built from the completed release **Vintage Wurlitzer 3D Lullaby Jukebox & Sleep Studio** at
<https://github.com/somphol/vintage-wurlitzer-lullaby-jukebox>, live at
<https://somphol.github.io/vintage-wurlitzer-lullaby-jukebox/>.

The reference release was built in one OpenCode session with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Its `state.json`
records the elapsed time and token counters.

## What is reused

- The working source (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`), manifest, tests and documentation in
  `assets/reference/` are the original work of this project, MIT licensed.
- Three.js 0.169.0 is MIT and is loaded from jsDelivr at runtime; it is not vendored.

## What is not included

- **No audio files.** `data/playlist.json` contains URLs to Wikimedia Commons recordings. Those
  recordings keep their source licences; the skill does not redistribute them.
- **No screenshots of third-party material.** The preview is an original render of the procedural
  cabinet.
- **No Wurlitzer assets or trademarks.** “Wurlitzer” is a trademark of its owner. The cabinet is an
  unaffiliated procedural tribute, not a licensed replica.

## Melodies

The 50 arrangements use public-domain melodies or original compositions; 18 are originals by
Wurlitzer Dream Division. The 32 archive performances are all marked Public domain on Wikimedia
Commons and are linked, not redistributed. Note selections, tempos, waveforms and envelopes are original to this
project. The four archive recordings are public domain; the skill links to them and redistributes no
audio.
