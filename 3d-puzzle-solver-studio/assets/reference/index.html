    Field guide

    How to play the 15-puzzle

    Slide one tile at a time into the empty slot. Order 1–15 left to right, top to bottom, with the gap in the bottom-right corner.

    01

    Move a tile

    Click or tap any tile touching the gap. It slides in with a soft thud. Arrow keys do the same from the keyboard.

    02

    Ask the solver

    Auto-solve runs A* with Manhattan distance plus linear conflicts. Step hint points at the next optimal tile.

    03

    Read the numbers

    h is the estimated distance to the goal; g is the moves you have made; Left is the remaining solution length.

    04

    Earn a record

    Best time and fewest moves are saved locally and only count when you solve without hints or auto-solve.
