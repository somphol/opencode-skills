02The Dial

Enamel,
fired twice.

Grand feu enamel is laid by hand and fired at 800 °C until it becomes glass. Beneath it, eleven applied indices in 18k gold catch whatever light the room will give. The hands are blued steel, shaped and polished one at a time.

03The Movement

Three hundred and twelve parts.
One heartbeat.

The Calibre 07 beats 28,800 times an hour and holds seventy-two hours of reserve. Every bridge is chamfered, every screw blued, every jewel set by hand — a finish you were never meant to see this closely.

04The Case

Forty-one millimetres
of quiet.

A case in 904L steel, cold-forged and finished over nine hours. The bezel is polished to a mirror; the flanks are brushed by hand. A domed sapphire crystal, 1.8 mm at its crown, sits almost flush with the dial.

05The Whole

Every part
returns.

Thirty-one jewels. Three hundred and twelve parts. One object, made to be worn for a lifetime and then handed on. The Calibre 07 is produced in sixty pieces a year.
