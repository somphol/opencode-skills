# Attribution

## The template is derived work

The San Francisco diorama in `assets/san-francisco-diorama` and the published build in
`results/san-francisco-diorama/index.html` derive from:

> **Manhattan Miniature 0.2.0** — an interactive React/TypeScript/three.js miniature of Manhattan,
> published by **SandBase Lab** (<https://www.sandbase.ai>).
> Obtained as the archive `manhattan-miniature-0.2.0.zip`.

### What was reused

- the scene mechanism: the road-graph-first city plan, block and parcel subdivision, instanced
  facade rendering, the ten building families, the camera/capture API and the virtual clock
- the single-file build pipeline (`vite` → inline JS/CSS → licence notices)
- the bilingual English / Simplified Chinese UI structure and the capture contract

### What is new in this fork

- all geography: the San Francisco peninsula outline, the rotated downtown grid, Market Street, the
  Embarcadero, Golden Gate Park, the Presidio and the bay piers
- eight landmark models (Golden Gate Bridge, Transamerica Pyramid, Salesforce Tower, Ferry Building,
  Coit Tower, Painted Ladies, City Hall, Alcatraz) and their camera views
- the `roofKit` roof detailing that replaced the New York water tanks
- bridge traffic derived from the landmark's yaw, and the fixed parcel/road clearance rules
- all English and Chinese copy for San Francisco
- `scripts/verify.mjs`, the frame-rate-independent interaction check, and this documentation

### Licence status

The original archive contained **no licence file** for the sample itself; only the third-party
notices of its npm dependencies (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`). Attribution is therefore given here, but
attribution is not a licence. If you intend to redistribute this template commercially, confirm the
terms with SandBase Lab first.

The original author's brand mark and wordmark are **not** redistributed: `src/skill-config.ts` uses a
neutral identity. The SandBase name appears only in this file and in the project history, as
attribution of origin — not as an endorsement of this fork.
