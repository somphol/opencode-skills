    How to explore

    Modes

    Drone orbits the skyline. Walk drops you onto the river promenade with drag-to-look and arrow keys or WASD. Transit follows a spline along the river and stops at each landmark.

    Light

    Midday, golden hour and neon night change the sky, sun, fog, water and every lit window.

    Keys

    123 modes · ↑←↓→ / WASD walk · Shift run · E inspect · Space pause tour · N next stop · L language · T time · M sound · H help

    Saving

    Visited stamps, notes and your route live in localStorage. Save Route downloads a portable JSON file; Load Route restores it on another device.

    Built with OpenCode and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Three.js r128. Geography is compressed and interpretive. GitHub ↗
