## Reused

- The working application source (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`), licence and third-party
  notices are copied into `assets/bangkok-3d-tour-guide/` as the reference implementation.
- Five original screenshots are included as visual references.
- The source documentation and session record were used to distill the method, lessons, verification,
  release-docs and build-log references.

The copied reference is **city-specific**. It is not a blank starter and must not be reskinned for
another city. Only the mechanism, control contracts and verification approach are intended to transfer.
